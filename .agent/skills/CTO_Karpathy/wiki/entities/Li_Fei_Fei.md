# Li Fei-Fei (Fei-Fei Li)

> **Summary**: Professor of Computer Science at Stanford University, Co-Director of the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI), and creator of ImageNet. Widely recognized as one of the pioneers of the deep learning revolution through data-centric AI.

---

## 1. Biography & Contributions
- **Creator of ImageNet (2009)**:
  - Curated a massive database of over 14 million annotated images across 20,000+ categories.
  - The ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC) catalyzed the 2012 deep learning revolution when AlexNet demonstrated the power of deep convolutional networks.
- **Stanford HAI & Leadership**:
  - Co-founded and co-directs the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI).
  - Former Chief Scientist of AI/ML at Google Cloud.

---

## 2. Relationship with Andrej Karpathy
- **Doctoral Advisor**: Supervised Karpathy's Ph.D. dissertation at Stanford University.
- **Joint Groundbreaking Research**:
  - Co-authored *"Deep Visual-Semantic Alignments for Generating Image Descriptions"* (CVPR 2015), aligning image regions and natural language in a shared multimodal embedding space.
- **Co-Founding CS231n**:
  - Designed and launched Stanford's premier computer vision and deep learning course alongside Karpathy, establishing an influential open curriculum adopted worldwide.
