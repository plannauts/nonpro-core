# Andrej Karpathy

> **Summary**: Leading AI researcher, engineer, and educator. Founding member of OpenAI, former Director of AI and Autopilot at Tesla, and founder of Eureka Labs. Widely revered for demystifying deep learning through first-principles education and minimalist codebases.

---

## 1. Biography & Key Milestones
- **Stanford University (Ph.D. in Computer Science)**:
  - Advised by Professor [[Li_Fei_Fei]].
  - Conducted breakthrough research in deep multimodal learning, aligning convolutional vision networks with recurrent language networks to achieve dense image captioning.
  - Designed and taught the legendary course **CS231n: Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition**, which trained the first generation of modern deep learning practitioners.
- **OpenAI (Founding Member & Research Scientist)**:
  - Contributed to early generative models, reinforcement learning, and Transformer architectures.
  - Co-developed [[OpenAI_Gym]] and authored *Pong from Pixels*, establishing [[Policy_Gradients]] as an accessible, end-to-end RL baseline.
- **Tesla (Director of AI & Autopilot Vision)**:
  - Spearheaded the transition of autonomous driving software from classical heuristics to massive, fleet-scale deep neural networks, practically implementing [[Software_2_0]].
- **OpenAI (Return to Foundation Research)**:
  - Worked on the GPT-4 generation of multimodal frontier models.
- **Eureka Labs (Founder, 2024)**:
  - Founded an AI-native education company building personalized AI teaching assistants to democratize high-level knowledge.

---

## 2. Influential Concepts & Mental Models
- **[[Software_2_0]]**: Programming via dataset curation and optimization of neural weights rather than human-written code.
- **[[Policy_Gradients]]**: Direct optimization of stochastic policies from raw sensor inputs, formalizing guess-and-check credit assignment.
- **[[LLM_OS]]**: Viewing LLMs as the CPU and kernel process of modern agentic computing systems.
- **[[LLM_Wiki]]**: Shifting from retrieval-heavy RAG to persistent, compilation-based personal knowledge bases.
- **[[Neural_Net_Training_Recipe]]**: Practical, defensive guidelines emphasizing data inspection, baselines, and 1-batch overfitting.
- **[[Character_Level_Language_Model]]**: Proving through `char-rnn` that next-character prediction induces complex structural representations.
- **[[First_Principles_AI]]**: *"What I cannot create, I do not understand."* Minimalist reference codebases (`micrograd`, `nanoGPT`, `llm.c`).
